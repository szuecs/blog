About Me

  • Nov 22, 2025
  • One minute read

This is my personal blog. One of my goals in 2026 is to write this blog about technology and opensource.

I am happy to discuss questions and details in Kubernetes or skipper HTTP proxy.

If you want to get in touch or discuss a blog post, please use Mastodon.
